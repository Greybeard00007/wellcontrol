Official IADC / IWCF Formula Cheat Sheet

1. Hydrostatic Pressure ($P_{hyd}$)
P_hyd (psi) = 0.052 × Mud Weight (ppg) × TVD (ft)
2. Formation Pore Pressure ($P_{form}$)
P_form = SIDPP + (0.052 × MW × TVD)
3. Kill Mud Weight (KMW)
KMW (ppg) = Current MW + [ SIDPP / (0.052 × TVD) ]
4. Initial Circulating Pressure (ICP)
ICP (psi) = SIDPP + SCR (Slow Circulating Rate)
5. Final Circulating Pressure (FCP)
FCP (psi) = SCR × (Kill Mud Weight / Current Mud Weight)
6. Max Allowable Surface Pressure (MAASP)
MAASP (psi) = (LOT EMW - Current MW) × 0.052 × Shoe TVD
7. Boyle's Ideal Gas Law (Migration Expansion)
P_1 × V_1 = P_2 × V_2 • (Pressure × Volume = Constant)

Key Well Control Golden Rules

• Pressure Takes TVD, Strokes Take MD: Hydrostatic balance relies solely on True Vertical Depth. Measured depth is used only to compute fluid displacement volumes and pump strokes.
• Follow the Drill Pipe Gauge: The drill string contains clean mud of known density. Annular pressure fluctuates wildly as gas expands. Keep drill pipe pressure locked on the step-down schedule.
• Respect Choke Lag Time: Pressure pulses travel through liquid mud at ~3,500 ft/s (approx. 2 seconds per 1,000 ft). Make small, measured choke adjustments and wait for needle response.
